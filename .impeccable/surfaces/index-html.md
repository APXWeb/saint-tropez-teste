# Surface: home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: someone deciding whether to go to Saint Tropez; secondary reader: the owners evaluating an APX Web proposal. Action: open the exact Google Maps place. Proof: the house's own photos, Google profile facts, Instagram bio rules. Constraints: no reservations, delivery, phone or order flows; only verified facts from src/data/site.js. Redesign of 2026-10-14 in the test copy; quality reference: Famiglia Mancini Trattoria (cinematic scroll, masks, pinned stages), adapted, never copied. User chose: light porcelain + turquoise fields + night chapters; canvas relief removed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page walks through the house the way a visitor does: through the arches. The glass vault of the salão is a row of arches, and every photograph opens through an arch or a rising sill. Refuses the photo-hero-with-two-buttons and the grid of equal product cards.
OWN-WORLD: porcelain white ground (#f5f6f3), cocoa type (#2a1a14), turquoise canopy (#0cb1ad) as whole chapter fields carrying cocoa type, night petrol (#0b1f20) for the salão and the visit; Gloock 400 display at full scale, Figtree for everything practical; arch-topped windows, full-bleed photos, 1px cocoa rules, no background patterns.
STORY: this is a confeitaria (arches of cakes, bread, salgados), here is the vitrine category by category, here is the salão at night under glass, here are the house rules, here is the Instagram, here is how to get there today.
FIRST VIEWPORT: porcelain ground; "Um novo conceito em confeitaria." in Gloock ~6rem top-left; live status, address and ONE canopy button (Abrir no Google Maps) top-right beside it, so the arcade below stays whole; the arcade fills the rest of the viewport: five arches (doces, bolo, fachada, croissants, tortinhas; three on phones) standing on the floor, the outer ones cut by the screen edge so it reads as the salão vault, rising from the floor in a stagger. Signature: on scroll the stage pins and the centre arch (the facade) widens until the facade fills the screen, the side arches sliding away; the address line lands on the photo.
FORM: candidate 1 of 1, pinned by the user's brief, which fixed the structure and the bar in its own words: "Crie uma primeira seção marcante, com uma fotografia de destaque", "Transforme a apresentação dos produtos em uma experiência visual editorial", "Apresente o ambiente da confeitaria de maneira envolvente", Instagram and "Visite" sections, and "Use o projeto da Famiglia Mancini ... como referência de qualidade". The palette was the user's answer (light porcelain + turquoise + night). The arch motif is the builder's own choice, taken from the salão vault. seed: none (exception: concept-seed was not run because the brief pinned the direction; disclosed to the user)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Motion grammar

Arch and sill clip-path openings with the image settling from 1.15 scale; display lines rising out of masks; scrubbed depth parallax; three pinned stages (hero arch, salgados stack, salão crossfade); everything visible without JS and under reduced motion.
